About the Tour

A 10-day adventure through Egypt's greatest wonders

We've designed a flowing itinerary that takes you through three of Egypt's most magnificent cities — each with its own character, its own magic, and its own ancient secrets waiting to be unlocked.

You'll stand at the foot of the Pyramids, walk through temples older than history, and drift along the Nile as the sun sets over a landscape unchanged for millennia. Every detail handled. Every moment yours.

Meet Yahya

Born in Cairo. Trained in history.
Passionate about Egypt.

For over 15 years I've guided travelers from every corner of the world through the wonders of my homeland. I speak Arabic, English, French, and some Spanish — but the language I love most is the one that ancient Egypt carved into stone thousands of years ago.

I don't run tours. I share experiences. I take you where the tour buses don't stop, I tell you the stories the textbooks leave out, and I make sure you leave Egypt not just having seen it, but having truly felt it.
