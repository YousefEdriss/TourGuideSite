The Journey

A 10-Day Journey Through the Land of the Pharaohs

From the iconic Pyramids of Giza to the mystical temples of Abu Simbel, this is Egypt as it was meant to be experienced — not from behind a tour bus window, but from the streets, the sands, and the stories that shaped civilisation itself.

Cairo
Days 1–3

Pyramids of Giza at sunrise, the Great Sphinx, Egyptian Museum, Khan el-Khalili bazaar, and the historic Islamic quarter.

Luxor
Days 4–6

Valley of the Kings, Karnak Temple, Luxor Temple by night, West Bank tombs, and a traditional felucca sail on the Nile.

Aswan & Abu Simbel
Days 7–10

Philae Island, Aswan High Dam, Nubian villages, and the awe-inspiring temples of Abu Simbel at dawn.
