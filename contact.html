Frequently Asked

Questions About Yahya's Tours

Everything you need to know before booking your Egypt adventure.

What's included in the tour package? ▶
The full package includes international and domestic flights, 4-star hotels with daily breakfast, private air-conditioned transfers, all entrance tickets (Pyramids, Valley of the Kings, Abu Simbel, Egyptian Museum, and more), Yahya's full guiding services, and personal photography at every landmark.
How far in advance should I book? ▶
We recommend booking at least 4–8 weeks in advance, especially for peak season (October–April). However, Yahya can often accommodate shorter notice bookings — reach out and he'll do his best to make it work.
Is the tour suitable for families with children? ▶
Absolutely. Yahya has guided many families with children of all ages and tailors the pace and content to keep everyone engaged. Egypt is endlessly fascinating for curious young minds — the stories Yahya tells make history feel like an adventure.
Can the itinerary be customised? ▶
Yes — every tour Yahya runs is tailored to you. Whether you want a photography-focused trip, a honeymoon itinerary, a deep-dive into a specific dynasty, or a mix of culture and beach time, Yahya will shape the journey around your interests and timeline.
What is the best time of year to visit Egypt? ▶
The ideal season is October through April when temperatures are comfortable (15–28°C). Summer (May–September) is very hot, especially in Luxor and Aswan (40°C+), but tours still run. Yahya can advise on the best timing for your specific itinerary and preferences.
Is Egypt safe to visit? ▶
Egypt's main tourist areas are very safe and well-travelled by millions of visitors every year. As a local, Yahya knows every area, every current condition, and keeps all guests safe and comfortable throughout the journey. He'll brief you on everything you need to know before you arrive.
Do I need a visa to visit Egypt? ▶
Most nationalities can obtain a visa on arrival at Cairo International Airport for a fee, or apply for an e-Visa online before travel. Yahya will advise you on the visa process specific to your nationality when you book, and help you prepare all travel documentation.
Can Yahya cater to dietary requirements or accessibility needs? ▶
Yes. Yahya takes all dietary requirements seriously — vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher, allergies. He knows the best restaurants in every city and will ensure every meal suits your needs. For mobility or accessibility requirements, simply mention them when booking and the itinerary will be adapted accordingly.
